SLIDE 2: INTRODUCTION
-------------------------------------------------------
• Proteins are the molecular machines of life
• Each protein has a specific function determined by its sequence
• Over 200 million protein sequences exist in databases
• Less than 1% have experimentally verified functions
• Manual annotation is slow, expensive, and cannot scale
• Need: Fast computational methods for initial screening

Key Stat: UniProt adds ~40,000 new sequences monthly, but experimental validation takes years

-------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: PROBLEM STATEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------
Problem:
"The gap between sequenced proteins and functionally annotated proteins 
continues to grow exponentially, creating a critical bottleneck in 
biological research and drug discovery."

Challenges:
1. Experimental characterization costs $10,000-$50,000 per protein
2. Lab validation takes 6-24 months per protein
3. 99% of protein sequences lack verified function
4. Researchers waste time on redundant experiments

Our Solution:
Machine learning-based prediction tool that provides rapid first-pass 
functional classification to prioritize experimental work

-------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4: OBJECTIVES
-------------------------------------------------------
Primary Objective:
Develop an AI-assisted tool to predict protein functional class from 
amino acid sequence

Specific Objectives:
1. Extract meaningful numerical features from protein sequences
2. Train a classification model on curated UniProt data
3. Achieve ≥60% accuracy (significantly above random baseline of 25%)
4. Build a usable prediction tool with confidence scores

Features Used (24 Total):
• Amino Acid Composition: 20 features (frequency of each amino acid)
• Sequence Length: 1 feature
• Molecular Weight: 1 feature
• Isoelectric Point (pI): 1 feature
• GRAVY Score (hydrophobicity): 1 feature

Why Random Forest?
• Works well with small datasets
• Handles non-linear patterns
• Provides feature importance
• No hyperparameter drama
• Fast training

Per-Class Performance:

• Enzyme: 72% recall
• Regulatory: 70% recall

Top Predictive Features:
1. GRAVY Score (hydrophobicity)

3. Leucine content (AA_L)
